## Label shape

`ChecklistLabels` (Checklist.tsx): `dismiss: string`, `progress(completedCount, total)`,
`markComplete(itemTitle)`, `markNotComplete(itemTitle)`. `ChecklistLauncherLabels`
(ChecklistLauncher.tsx) extends it with `fabLabel(title, completedCount, total)` and
`dismissed(title)`. Same convention as `StepPopoverLabels`: typed interface, module-level
`DEFAULT_*` constant, `labels?: Partial<...>` prop, merged as `{ ...DEFAULT, ...labels }`.
Interpolating entries are functions, not strings, because word order around a count or a
title is not fixed across languages. `ChecklistLauncher` merges its own labels then passes
the merged object straight through as `Checklist`'s `labels` prop.
